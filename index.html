How it works

  1. Walk in on your way to work.
  2. Pick up coffee, flowers and a card, ready together.
  3. Write a few words and hand it over.

Around €12 for all three. Pickup only.

Why pick it up instead of ordering delivery?

Delivery is great when you can't be there. LuKat is for the mornings you can.

Delivery

You order ahead and choose a time slot. It arrives even when you're not there.

LuKat

You decide that morning. You pick it up on your way and hand it over yourself.

So LuKat isn't for surprising someone across town. It's for the person you'll see before work: at home, on the walk, or at the office door.
